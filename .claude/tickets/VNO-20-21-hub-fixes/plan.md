## Added 2026-10-23, fourth round (hub production: Flow and ComfyUI books at the same time)

Keep `max_parallel` meaning what it means for model calls. Test with the scheduler: a queued ComfyUI image job and a Flow image job both run at once; two ComfyUI jobs still run one at a time; two Flow jobs one at a time.
